# Video Walkthrough Script (target 7:00, limit 5–8 min)

**Setup:** screen-share the submission markdown (or the architecture diagram), camera on if comfortable. Speak, do not read. Use these as talking points. Record at 1080p, upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**, test the link in a private window, then paste it into the portal.

| Time | Section | What to say (talking points) |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:30** | Intro | "I'm Abdullah. I'll walk through how I'd operate as Ajaia's IT Manager: incident response, the infrastructure design, security and compliance decisions, how I used AI, and the tradeoffs. My goal is judgment and prioritization, not a tool list." |
| **0:30–2:00** | **Incident response** | "Four issues, but not equal. **Key exposure first**, because it's active and exploitable. **Permissions second**, because they're the bigger latent risk to client data. **AI governance third**: I publish a one-line interim rule today. **Onboarding fourth, but it's a multiplier** with 25 new hires. For the key: assume compromised, **rotate first, clean up later**, check provider logs for abuse, set spend caps, scan for sibling secrets, turn on push protection. If client data was reachable, I bring in counsel for the HIPAA breach assessment, I don't decide that alone. For permissions I **restrict first, clean later**. No mass deletion. Communication is blame-free, fast, and one source of truth." |
| **2:00–3:30** | **System design** | *(show the diagram)* "Identity is the control plane: Google Workspace as the single IdP with SSO and group-based roles. Four data classes, L0 to L3. Regulated data lives in a separate zone with named access, encryption and full logging. All AI traffic goes through an **AI gateway**: central keys, logging, redaction, model allow-list and spend caps. That directly fixes the key-leak root cause. Compliance evidence is automated through a platform like Vanta, logs go to a central store." |
| **3:30–5:00** | **Security & compliance** | "Access tiers: standard, project-based, just-in-time elevated, and privileged with separate admin accounts. Devices: MDM, encryption, EDR, posture-gated access, no regulated data on laptops. Top risks: **AI data leakage**, **account takeover**, **access sprawl**. For compliance I'm honest about what each is: HIPAA is a law with no certification, I need BAAs and a risk analysis. FERPA works via the school-official exception. SOC 2 is an auditor's attestation: Type I first, Type II after an observation window. I build **one control set mapped to all three**. Violations are prevented by DLP and defaults, detected through logs and drift alerts, and handled by a breach runbook." |
| **5:00–6:00** | **AI in operations + ticketing** | "Three AI workflows: an IT support assistant in Slack, an onboarding assistant, and an automated weekly security and access audit. The rule is the same for all: **AI recommends and drafts, humans approve anything touching access or regulated data**. Ticketing: Slack-first intake, priority by impact times urgency, security and regulated-data tickets auto-P1, with SLAs. I measure reopen rate so I know if the AI is closing tickets badly." |
| **6:00–6:40** | **My AI workflow** | "I used Claude to draft structure and tables quickly, and as a coverage checklist. I overrode it on [ordering of priorities], [softening compliance claims], and [limiting automation]. The decisions on prioritization, where humans stay in the loop, and the honest compliance timeline are mine." *(Replace with your real overrides.)* |
| **6:40–7:15** | **Tradeoffs + close** | "Tradeoffs: Google Workspace over Okta for now, to move fast, revisit at scale. Restrict-first over cleanup. A compliance platform over spreadsheets: costs money, saves months. JIT access adds friction for a few people but shrinks blast radius. In my first 30 days: stabilise, inventory, build onboarding and ticketing first, then governance and the AI gateway. Thanks for watching." |

## Delivery tips
- Practise once with a timer. Aim for **6:30–7:15** so you never risk the 8-minute cap.
- Say *why* for each decision. That is what is being scored.
- If you stumble, keep going. Natural beats polished.
- Open the video with the one-sentence priority logic. It shows judgment immediately.
- Before submitting: watch it fully once, check audio, confirm the link is **Unlisted** (not Private) and opens in a private browser window.
