# Paperwright — Business Model

*Research, written right.* A team-based research-writing and publication-support studio.

> **Figures below are planning assumptions, not market data.** Prices, volumes and costs are starting hypotheses for you to test with your first 10–20 clients. Replace them with real numbers as you learn.

---

## 1. What we sell (and what we don't)

**We sell expertise applied to the client's own work:** structure, argument, methodology review, statistics, editing, formatting, submission support, and feedback that teaches.

**We do not** write papers or assignments for clients to submit as their own, take exams, fabricate data, or guarantee acceptance or grades. Putting this in writing is a *brand asset* (it is what lets universities, labs and supervisors recommend you) and the main legal and reputational protection for the team.

| Service line | Deliverable | Typical client |
|---|---|---|
| **Manuscript Check** (entry) | 20-min call + 1-page reviewer-style summary of top issues | Anyone; free/low-cost lead magnet |
| **Research Paper Support** | Structure map, argument & methodology review, line editing, citation/style clean-up | Grad / PhD students |
| **Technical Paper Support** | Methods & results clarity, figure/table/equation QA, spec and reproducibility review | Engineers, labs, startups |
| **Journal Submission Pack** | Journal-fit shortlist, formatting to author guidelines, cover letter, ethics/data statements, reviewer-response coaching | Researchers submitting |
| **Literature Review Sprint** | Search strategy, source matrix, synthesis outline (client writes the synthesis) | Thesis / review-article writers |
| **Data & Statistics Help** | Test selection, analysis, interpretation, reproducible figures | Any empirical project |
| **Assignment Coaching** | Rubric breakdown, outline feedback, draft critique, referencing, study sessions | Undergrad / Master's students |
| **Lab / Department Plan** | Monthly retainer: priority turnaround, shared style guide, workshops | Labs, departments, bootcamps |

## 2. Lean canvas

| Block | Paperwright |
|---|---|
| **Problem** | 1) Good research is rejected for weak structure, unclear contribution or format errors. 2) Students lack feedback on how to write, not just what to fix. 3) Hiring a trustworthy expert is hard; the market is full of ghost-writing mills. |
| **Customer segments** | PhD / Master's students · early-career researchers · engineers & startups with white papers · labs and departments · bootcamps and tutoring centres |
| **Unique value proposition** | Reviewer-style feedback from subject-matched editors, with a clear integrity policy; you stay the author and learn the craft. |
| **Solution** | Tiered services (Check → Support → Submission Pack → Retainer), fixed-scope pricing, 48-hour first feedback. |
| **Channels** | LinkedIn and Instagram content, YouTube how-tos, university and lab partnerships, supervisor referrals, SEO guides ("how to format for [journal]"), Upwork/Fiverr-style marketplaces for first reviews |
| **Revenue streams** | Per-project fees · packages · lab retainers · workshops · templates and checklists (digital products) |
| **Cost structure** | Contributor pay (largest, ~55–65 % of project revenue) · tools (reference manager, plagiarism & grammar checks, project tool) · marketing · payments & admin · professional indemnity insurance |
| **Key metrics** | Manuscript Checks booked → paid conversion · on-time delivery % · repeat/referral rate · gross margin per project · average turnaround · client-reported improvement score |
| **Unfair advantage** | Subject-matched reviewer network, integrity-first reputation, a growing library of journal-specific formatting playbooks |

| Technical Paper Support | Up to 8,000 words incl. figures/equations QA | $250–$600 |
| Journal Submission Pack | Format + cover letter + statements (paper already written) | $150–$350 |
| Reviewer-Response Coaching | Up to 1 reviewer round | $120–$300 |

**Pricing rules:** fixed scope, written quote before work starts; 50 % upfront for projects over $150; rush (≤ 72 h) +30 %; student discount through verified `.edu`/university email; never discount below contributor cost + 20 %.

### Sample unit economics (one Research Paper Support project)
| | |
|---|---|
| Price | $300 |
| Contributor pay (≈ 60 %) | −$180 |
| Tools, payment fees (≈ 7 %) | −$21 |
| **Gross margin** | **$99 (≈ 33 %)** |

At ~8 projects/month that is ≈ $800 gross margin before marketing and admin. The path to a healthy business is **retainers and repeat clients** (lower acquisition cost, higher margin) rather than one-off projects.

| **Client Success & Intake** | Free checks, scoping, quotes, deadlines, client communication |

| **Formatting & Submission Specialist** | Journal guidelines, reference styles, cover letters, submission portals |

| **QA Reviewer** | Second read on every deliverable; integrity and plagiarism checks |

Start lean: Lead + Intake + 2–3 contract editors + 1 methods specialist; add roles as volume proves demand.

## 5. Delivery workflow (every project)

1. **Intake** — form: discipline, document type, target journal/rubric, deadline, current draft, goal. Integrity acknowledgement signed.
2. **Scope & quote** — fixed price, deliverables, rounds, turnaround; deposit.
3. **Match** — assign subject editor (+ methods/formatting specialist if needed).
4. **First review (≤ 48 h)** — reviewer-style report: contribution, structure, method, evidence, writing, format; top-5 priorities.
5. **Revise together** — client revises; editor line-edits and coaches (tracked changes + comments, never silent rewrites).
6. **QA & integrity check** — second read, citation verification, similarity check on client's own text.
7. **Submit-ready handoff** — final file, checklist, short "what we changed and why" note.
8. **Follow-up** — satisfaction score, referral ask, reviewer-response offer after journal feedback arrives.

**Targets (assumptions):** 10 paying clients by day 45 · ≥ 40 % Manuscript Check → paid conversion · ≥ 90 % on-time delivery · 2 retainer clients by day 90.

## 7. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Seen as an essay mill** | Public integrity policy, coaching-first language, tracked-change delivery, refuse out-of-policy requests and say so |
| **Quality varies by editor** | Subject matching, QA second read, standard report template, quarterly calibration |
| **Confidentiality (unpublished data)** | NDA in contract, access-controlled storage, no uploading client text to public AI tools, delete files on request |
| **Name / trademark conflict** | Search and register before launch; the generator makes a rename a one-line change |
| **Feast-and-famine demand (deadline seasons)** | Retainers, off-season workshops and templates, a flexible contractor bench |
| **Over-promising outcomes** | Never promise acceptance or grades; promise process and turnaround |
| **Legal / tax** | Written contractor agreements; confirm local registration, invoicing and tax obligations with a local accountant |
