# Prologue
## The Last Light

The night my mother died, the sea was black.

Not dark blue, not silver beneath the moon, but black—the color of ink spilled across a page before the words could be written.

I remember the storm.

I remember the lighthouse burning above the cliffs.

And I remember a woman walking toward it with blood on her hands.

She wore my mother's face.

I was twelve years old when I saw her disappear into the rain.

For twelve years, I told myself it had been a dream.

Then, on the night I returned to Vale House, someone left a letter beneath my bedroom door.

The handwriting was unmistakable.

My mother's.

*My darling Eleanor,*

*If you are reading this, then the sea has finally returned what I tried to bury.*

*Do not trust the man who promises to protect you.*

*Do not trust the man who tells you he loves you.*

*And whatever you do, do not go to the lighthouse after midnight.*

*Forgive me.*

*Mother*

I read the letter three times.

Then I looked at the clock.

Eleven fifty-seven.

Outside, the lighthouse beam swept across the bedroom wall.

Once.

Twice.

And then, for the first time in twelve years, it went dark.
