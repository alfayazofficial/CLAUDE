# The Tide Keeps Our Names — Story Bible

Gothic romantic tragedy · 1891 · fictional Cornish island · target 90,000 words · 30 chapters + prologue + epilogue
Narration: alternating first person (Eleanor / Gabriel), occasional Adrian chapters.

## Cast
- **Eleanor Vale (30)** pianist, returns after 12 years away.
- **Gabriel Thorne (32)** lighthouse keeper, ex-naval surgeon. Brother Samuel lost on the *Halcyon*.
- **Adrian Ashcombe (34)** magistrate, Eleanor's fiancé. Holds the second lighthouse key.
- Margaret Vale (mother, diary), Dr. Silas Reed, Mrs. Hester Crowe, Samuel Thorne, Rev. Blackwood.

## Continuity facts established so far
- Anniversary of the mother's death: lighthouse light goes out each year at ~11:52 p.m. (logbook entry; originally "by order of the Magistrate").
- Two lighthouse keys: Gabriel's and the magistrate's (Adrian). Last night's blackout was "not by order of the Magistrate."
- Gabriel came six years ago (after keeper Pennick drowned); Gabriel has carried one of Margaret's pearls for twelve years.
- Gabriel knew Margaret's face and was the last person to hear her voice. He knows of a second letter Margaret left behind (to be paid off).
- Mrs. Crowe warns Eleanor away from Gabriel. Wreck: the *Halcyon*, off the Mare's Teeth.
- Margaret hated white roses; Adrian brought white roses.

## RESOLVED (decisions made for the full draft)
- Eleanor was **18** when her mother died; she left the island that winter (12 years ago); she is now 30. Gabriel was 20, Adrian 22.
- Gabriel is from the mainland. He spent one winter on the island as Dr. Reed's apprentice. Margaret Vale was his patron and paid for his medical studies. Eleanor never met him (Margaret kept her away from the surgery).

## THE TRUTH (secret architecture)
- **The Ashcombe wrecking.** For generations the Ashcombes (with the Vales profiting) darkened the lighthouse on stormy nights so ships broke on the Mare's Teeth, then salvaged cargo. Lord Ashcombe (Adrian's father) was magistrate. The lighthouse key is held by the magistrate.
- **Margaret Vale** found the wrecking ledger in the abbey crypt and began secretly recording the drowned (names carved on the tidal wall below the crypt, visible at spring low tide). Her husband **Josiah Vale** betrayed her: he told Lord Ashcombe she had the ledger.
- **The night (Sept 28, 12 years ago).** Lord Ashcombe darkened the light for the brig *Sparrowhawk*. Margaret, hands bleeding from forcing the crypt door (Gabriel dressed them), climbed to the lighthouse to relight. Ashcombe struck her on the gallery; she went over the rail. Gabriel caught her wrist. Ashcombe threatened to hang Gabriel's brother Samuel (caught stealing salvage) if he held on. Gabriel hesitated; Margaret pulled free: "Tell Eleanor." Adrian (22) watched from the stair, then went down the rocks and tried to pull her from the water (blood on his hands: what Eleanor saw). Reed signed "accidental drowning". 14 men of the *Sparrowhawk* drowned.
- **Gabriel's sin:** he let go (or did not hold harder), then took Ashcombe's money for his studies and went to sea. He keeps one of Margaret's pearls and her **second letter** (addressed to Eleanor, sealed) which he was to deliver and never did.
- **Mrs. Crowe** kept Margaret's first letter and slipped it under Eleanor's door (the "mother's letter").
- **Samuel Thorne is alive.** Survived the *Halcyon* (six years ago; Adrian, now magistrate, again ordered the light dark, for a smuggler's run, and the *Halcyon* struck). Rev. Blackwood hides him in the abbey. Samuel wrote the second note and darkened the light this year (copy of Pennick's key) to draw Eleanor and Gabriel together.
- **Annual ritual:** each year Adrian sends a written order that the light be put out 11:52 p.m. to 1:14 a.m. (penance). This year he did not.
- **Adrian** loves Eleanor, watched his father kill her mother, buried it, later let his father die in the Penhallow Manor fire (the "ashes"). He keeps Eleanor close to keep the truth buried and her safe.

## ENDING
Storm. Rev. Blackwood and Samuel sail at dawn with the ledger, Reed (held by Adrian, freed) to the Assizes. Adrian burns Margaret's diary and second letter, then darkens the lamp as their boat nears the Teeth. Eleanor and Gabriel fight their way up and relight; a surge sweeps both from the gallery. The boat is saved. Adrian is tried. The tide wall shows the names, and two more.
