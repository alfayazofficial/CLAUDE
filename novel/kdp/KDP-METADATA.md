## Book description (blurb, ~170 words, paste into KDP)

Some secrets are buried beneath the sea. Others are buried in the hearts of those we love.

Cornwall, 1891. Eleanor Vale returns to the island of her childhood to bury the past. On her first night home, someone slides a letter beneath her door in her dead mother's handwriting: *Do not trust the man who promises to protect you.*

She is promised to Adrian Ashcombe, the island's charming magistrate. But the lighthouse keeper, Gabriel Thorne, a former naval surgeon with gray eyes and a guarded heart, knows more about the night her mother died than he will say. Drawn to the cliffs, the crypt beneath the ruined abbey, and a ledger of the drowned, Eleanor finds love where she should find only danger.

As the truth rises with the tide, three people discover that the truth can be more destructive than any lie, and that some debts can only be paid in the sea.

**A slow-burn gothic romance of secrets, sacrifice, and a love that does not get a happy ending.**
