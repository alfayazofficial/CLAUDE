# Chapter Outline — 60 Chapters, 5 Acts

Legend: **FE** = Fayaz & Ermis thread (S30). **Torn page** = unfinished story breaks mid-chapter. Register numbers appear as `#0713`.
Each chapter: ~3,000–4,500 words. Each opens with a short epigraph from a Register letter, closes with a Keeper's margin note (optional).

Fools (comic characters who speak truth by accident): Act I — Pereira the lamp-seller (Lisbon) and Quaye the taxi-chorus (Accra); Act II — Sema the ferry-café cook (Istanbul) and Fergal the pub-seanchaí (Galway); Act III — Bunny, a Dhaka tea-stall boy who gives Fayaz life advice, and Ayu, Ermis's grandmother's parrot; Act IV — Old Zuhura (Cairo clinic cleaner) and Mr. Pennywhistle (Manchester band-room caretaker); Act V — Captain Dag's parrot-less crew (Oslo) and Mrs. Trelawny (Cornwall post-office gossip).
