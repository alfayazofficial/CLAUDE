# Continuity notes, Chapters 45-50

## Timeline / season
- Ch 45 is the first week of July (Kyoto and Gothenburg); Ch 46 is August (Bonwire, Soweto, Medellin Feria de las Flores); Ch 47 is August (Isfahan, Baghdad, Dhaka monsoon); Ch 48 runs July to September; Ch 49 is the last week of October (Sapporo first snow, Manchester wedding, Kano henna night); Ch 50 spans the Mid-Autumn full moon (Hoi An), then a November Saturday (Accra).
