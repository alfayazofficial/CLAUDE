# Continuity notes, Chapters 8-13

## Ch 8 (Bergen, S07)
- Lars's boat: the *Ragnhild*, 11 m, tarred oak; debt to bank 412,000 kroner; engine failing since spring.
- Kristian Strand (78, retired waiter, Bryggen restaurant for 51 years) is Lars's stepfather (married Lars's late mother; she died six winters ago); Lars calls him "Far"; Kristian calls him "the young master"; carries a napkin on his arm.
- Marit Strand (38, train guard) is Kristian's daughter, i.e. Lars's step-sister and oldest friend.
- Henrik Strand (31, surgeon) is Sigrid's best friend since age nine; cannot use a telephone, communicates on marker-pen sheets of paper.
- Sigrid works at the Institute of Marine Research (Nordnes); charters Lars's boat weekly (Tuesdays) for a cod-tagging study; has done so about six months.
- Grant letter: coastal cod grant ends 31 March, not renewable; offered 14-month post at the Arctic station at Ny-Alesund, Svalbard; must accept by 15 January. Sigrid has told no one (Henrik guessed).
- Solveig Dahl (36, umbrella maker from Alesund) sells "UNBREAKABLE" umbrellas at the Fisketorget; gave Sigrid a plum-coloured umbrella with "Look up. It is only weather." stitched in the lining.
- Espen Lund (49, poet) chalks daily weather poems outside the harbour cafe and volunteers on the Sea Rescue boat that tows the Ragnhild in.
- Chapter date: a Tuesday in the last week of November.

## Ch 9 (Kochi, S08)
- Shop: Karayil Books ("On the Shore"), Fort Kochi; Arjun's father Hari Panicker (76, retired butcher) keeps his old brass butcher's scale on the counter.
- The book: *The Schoolchild's Annual*, bought by Meera every 26 November (she wears a green sari that day). Arjun slips the SAME poem (pencil, onion-skin paper, Malayalam script, unsigned) between pages 41 and 42 of her copy, re-copying it fresh each year; this is the 7th year (first when he was 20).
- Neither has ever spoken of it. Meera suspects/knows it is his; Arjun does not know that she knows.
- Poem text is given in Ch 9 (3 quatrains; last word "Understand"). In the flood the rain smudged the last line of Meera's copy; she says she "always knew what it was" and thinks "it is time somebody said it out loud" (set-up for Ch 21).
- Meera teaches Class Six English at a school in Mattancherry; pupil Sreeja Nambiar (14) is the class librarian and sharp-eyed.
- Flood on 26 Nov (north-east monsoon, high tide in Princess Street); about 4,000 books saved in the loft; the lower shelves (cookbooks, Marxist theory) ruined.
- Gopal Varma (58, vet/matchmaker) withdrew the Kottayam girl's horoscope.
- Sreeja Iyer (55, plumber) and Vishnu Kurup (45, rickshaw puller, Meera's friend) both appear.

## Ch 10 (Sao Paulo, S09; Osaka, S03)
- Hospital: Hospital Santa Aurora (fictional). Carlos's late wife died "two Marches ago" of a long illness (name not given); ring worn 3 years.
- Invented patient: Raimundo Teles, 58, bus driver from Santos, fell from scaffolding; Carlos rehabbed him 14 months; walked 300 m into the hospital on the morning of his aneurysm surgery and died on Beatriz's table at 10:04, three weeks before the chapter. Grandson Leandro Ribeiro (16) brought pao de queijo to Carlos.
- Beatriz is a vascular surgeon; kept her late mother's surname Lima; father Pedro Rocha (60, retired chef) keeps a food blog; he photographed Hiroshi's counter in Osaka in spring.
- Beatriz and Carlos were stuck in a lift (locksmith Thiago Oliveira stalled 40 min deliberately). She saw the ring and believes he is married; she asked to come to the rehab wing "on Thursday for a follow-up". She kept his handkerchief.
- Hiroshi's shop: Tanabe, eight stools, Fukushima ward, Osaka; the bowl is set at the third stool from the left near the steam pipe (Mei's stool, every Tuesday for six years). Mei lost her sense of taste in spring and has not been back since March; the Brazilian blog photo (140,000 likes) shows her stool. She resolved to go "on Tuesday" but has not gone (Ch 16 picks up). Daichi Ito (historian) brought Hiroshi the news; Hiroshi said only "Eat."
- Time-zone note: Sao Paulo is 12 hours behind Osaka.

## Ch 11 (Lagos, S10)
- Building: Adeniran Court, Yaba (4 floors used; 4th floor AJO, 3rd floor Chidinma's atelier); shared generator, diesel levy committee.
- Emeka's company: AJO, a phone app based on the ajo/esusu savings circle; 9 employees (youngest Tobi, 24); outstanding debt after payroll 4.1 million naira; sold his watch; loan against a flat in Surulere. Employees know, and have secretly agreed to keep working.
- Chidinma's label/dress form: the mannequin is called "Her"; she wore the sharp-shouldered indigo adire jacket in the finale; show held in the lobby during a power cut after the venue deposit (2.3m naira) fell through; her father is a retired tailor in Enugu who calls her "Nnem".
- Ifeoma Bello (60, from Ibadan, ex-first-mate on freighters) is Emeka's mentor/investor; Ngozi Afolabi (33) is Chidinma's best-friend mechanic; Mama Ngozi Nwosu (59) leads a small chapel in Yaba and prophesies ("lose everything Thursday, gain it Friday").
- Chidinma paid Emeka's share of the generator levy (4,000 naira) without telling him.

## Ch 12 (Lyon S11; Krakow S12; Lisbon S01)
- Pierre's late wife: Odile (died 4 years ago in spring after 11 months of illness); her walnut-rye-cumin loaf baked every Sunday; Pierre gives 12 loaves away via Baptiste Lefevre (13). Bakery on the Croix-Rousse.
- Margaux: shop "Les Violettes", Rue de la Martinière; late husband Luc, a violin-maker, died 3 years ago; leaves Parma violets with grey ribbon on Pierre's step at 3:30 a.m. on Sundays (11 weeks). Pierre now knows it is "the florist, Madame Laurent" (saw her walking away); he sent a 13th loaf to her via Baptiste. Her brother (20, called Baptiste Vidal) helps in the shop.
- Zofia's grandfather's silver minute-repeater pocket watch (stopped 11:52 four years ago), engraved "Zofia, for her birth"; Jakub repaired it and collected-day was 29 November; she cannot hear the top notes in her left ear (noticed in September); Jakub noticed she follows his hands. First duet: old Polish lullaby on the shop's walnut pianino. Shop on Floriańska Street.
- Zofia plays for coins at the foot of St. Mary's on Thursdays at dusk in a thin red scarf.
- Lisbon: Tomás proposed by letter addressed to "Senhora Inês Carvalho, Rua das Flores"; Inês delivered it to herself and signed it "Yes. Delivered. — I.C."; engagement ring is a copper-wire loop from a tram motor; her sister Rita (journalist, 32) was the one who posted the original letter (Ch 1/2 letter); Nuno Pereira (27, mechanic) is Tomás's friend; Pereira sold Inês a small lamp.
- Keeper's note at the close of Act I: "The winter will ask for payment."

## Ch 13 (Istanbul S13; Accra S02) [first chapter of Act II]
- Lines: Yilmaz Line (red, ferry Hizir) and Kaya Line (blue, ferry Nilufer), both run Uskudar-Karakoy; feud began with a fog grounding in winter 1996; both fathers swore oaths on the pier. Council licence (10 years) up for award; both families bid.
- Ahmed's father: Captain Suleyman Yilmaz (58, invented). Old engineer Kerem Aydin (73) raised Ahmed with the captain. Elif's father Recep Kaya is dead (4 years); her uncle (mother's brother) Mehmet Sahin (63, radio-man of the Kaya Line) raised her and runs the line; he "trusts the wrong people".
- Elif has written ~173 poems on the backs of tickets/simit bags; carried by Burak Aydin (14, simit seller, bookish, paid in books). Ahmed answers by whistling a Black Sea shepherd tune at dawn and has written one line back ("I cannot write as you do. I can only say that I hear it. - A."). They have never spoken. Twenty paces separate their piers.
- Sema (51, ferry-café cook at the Eminonu landing, 28 years, "no quarrel inside, leave it on the hook") and husband Kerem Arslan (44, grocer who believes newspapers).
- Accra: Oxford Street, Osu; Owusu Pharmacy won Greater Accra Pharmacy of the Year on 9 December; Mensah Cabs depot is 12 metres east. Abena publicly said she knows who paid her father's debt and is "waiting"; Kwame has not yet spoken. Quaye is 70, Kwame's oldest driver (19 years), gold tooth, flat cap, football shirt. Adwoa Tetteh (44, kiosk owner) is Abena's friend; Nii Tetteh (43) leads the choir.
- Abena's father is dead ("not here to see" the scales).
