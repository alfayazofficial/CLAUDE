# Continuity notes — Chapters 33–38

## Ch 33 — The Jeepney Night
- S33 is tragic: Gabriel Santos dies in the early hours after Typhoon Isidra (signal 3, out of season, second week of May); he made five rescue trips from the flooded Pandacan roofs, put seven people safely on a dry ramp at the underpass, then the water took the jeepney. Found at 9 a.m.
- Jeepney is named *Hiraya*; route Santa Mesa–Guadalupe; Santo Niño on dashboard; rear panel painted by Gabriel's late father: "SAKAY NA, MAY ORAS PA" (Get on, there is still time).
- Biscuit tin under dashboard holds 197 coins, every fare Maricel ever paid, none spent. Joy Aquino carried it out and holds it at end of Ch 33; Maricel has NOT opened it (available for Ch 43 funeral).
- Gabriel's last words, relayed by Joy: "Tell the girl in the front seat she left her change" (sukli = what is left over).
- Last texts: Gabriel "Hintay ka lang diyan. Papunta na ako." and "Isang trip pa, tapos diyan na ako. May sasabihin ako." Maricel's unsent draft: "Sabihin mo na ngayon." (left unsent on her phone).
- Maricel's phone alias is "Marcy" (company: Northwind Broadband, 31st floor tower, agents told to shelter in place). Her caller Walt Halvorsen, 81, Duluth, Minnesota, widower of 54-year marriage; she said she would "tell him tomorrow"; Walt told her "tell him tonight."
- Maricel rode the front seat for seven months; her mother is dead (grave); sister writes poems.
- Regular passengers: Joy Aquino (librarian, Sampaloc branch), Ramon Garcia (weaver, matchmaker), Rey Bautista (77, rides to the cemetery gate nightly), Grace Ocampo (11, outed the romance aloud).
- S23: Yusuf's shop is in the tailors' lane behind Kurmi Market, Kano. Gown: ivory Swiss lace, 40 nights' work; hidden black five-point star stitched inside the hem. Wedding was due "in a fortnight" in Zaria to Aminu Danjuma (baker, 43).
- Hauwa has delivered 412 babies. She declared she will not marry Aminu Danjuma; she will tell him herself tomorrow with her father (Sani Yakubu) beside her. Dress "not for sale"; Yusuf must ask properly via his mother and Hajiya Zainab Bello (matchmaker, 69). Ch 49 can stage the proper proposal/wedding.
- Two Aminus in comic play: Aminu Danjuma (groom, baker) and Aminu Yakubu (goldsmith, 50, neighbour in the lane).

## Ch 34 — Puppets Without Strings
- Petr's theatre: the Theatre of the Golden Hare, lane behind the Church of Our Lady beneath the Chain, Malá Strana; 63 seats; seat F7 (aisle, 2nd row from back) kept empty for 14 months since Lucie's review. Show: "Bez nitě" (Without Strings). Petr has made 312 puppets in 18 years.
- Lucie's review line: "A man who speaks only through wood has, for all his eloquence, nothing to say... Their maker is not in the room." Subscribers halved; landlord Kučera gave notice but the theatre survived.
- Both read each other's Register entries (0418 and 0227) in November; each knew the other's hand.
- Petr spoke in his own voice for the first time on stage: "Děkuji, že jste přišli" (Thank you for coming). Lucie left a note and a too-large coin: "I was wrong. Not about the wood. About the man. — L.H."
- Markéta Veselý (45) runs the cloakroom, ex-Podolí lifeguard; Vojtěch (cobbler brother, "soles and souls"); Jana (15, niece, stagehand).
- S24: Eleni left George thirty years ago (aged 28); she is a widow of six years; shop is "STAVROU — HERBS, HONEY, & EVERYTHING ELSE" behind Chania's Venetian harbour. George's thirty annual letters were returned to him unopened (by a niece, one winter morning); he brought the whole bundle back to her tied in kitchen string. They exchanged mantinada couplets; she opened the first letter (April, the year she left) that night: "The door is on its hinges, either way." Eleni has NOT yet answered him beyond "I'll send for you."
- Kostas Mavros (taxi), Katerina Nikolaidis (12, minds the till).

## Ch 35 — The Lighthouse Keeper's Son
- Galle light: 136 steps, 12-second beam. Anil's father (blind, kept the light 38 years, called Mr. Perera) and grandfather D. Perera (keeper in the 1960s–70s).
- Behind the lens pedestal: a biscuit tin with 71 airmail letters from Seraphine to D. Perera, 1964–1979 (return address: Miss S. Wyndham-Rao, Mrs. Alder's rooms, Kentish Town). First (summer 1964, aged 18): "A light does not reply, it only goes on being there." Last (1979) describes beginning the ledger, with a P.S.: "If any of my blood should come to your door one day, needing to see further than she can, let her up the stairs."
- Seraphine stood at the lamp-room rail at age 12. Nadeesha has been home in Galle three weeks (April); the Register has been open five months; she has read ~4,000 letters of thanks and ~1,100 of rage.
- Anil and Nadeesha's hands rest together on the lens rail; nothing spoken. Chaminda Abeywardena (newsagent, hoards papers) and Kumari Senanayake (15, jasmine seller) are witnesses. Sampath is Nadeesha's brother, tea seller.
- Kyoto: Kenji gave Haruka a blank (expressionless) young-woman Noh mask of pale cypress, wrapped in purple silk, four months' work, with a tanka inside the silk. She said it is "the first that lets me stop." Daichi Kobayashi (apprentice, tea-drinker), Yuto Shimizu (Arashiyama boatman, fooled by a sushi chopping board, 400,000 yen).

## Ch 36 — Cycling Through Cloud
- Stage Six of the Vuelta over Las Palmas (Medellín). Daniela's farm: Finca La Nube. 40,000 unsold roses laid along an 800 m verge after a Miami buyer cancelled; bank wants 90 million pesos by end of harvest or takes the farm.
- Sebastián crashed on a descent in the Tour of the Andes eleven months ago after a flower-stall glimpse made him think of Daniela; first met a year ago at her gate (puncture mended with a teaspoon, gave him a rose for his mother). He finished 11th (prize 400,000 pesos). Daniela's answer: "Not now" is not "no"; ask again at end of harvest. She gave him the last rose "for your mother."
- Radio commentator Esteban Ortiz (Cali). Paola Ramírez (10).
- S12 is tragic: Zofia's final recital in the hall of the Collegium Maius (Bach Chaconne), deaf for nine weeks; Jakub tapped the pulse on an oak beam under the stage trapdoor. Her grandfather's silver pocket watch engraved "Z.K." (Jakub secretly repaired it and left it on her doorstep in January). Surgery on 9 June; she did not wake; Jakub winds the watch at 9 p.m. every night for life.
- S26 torn page: Circus of the Silver Mare, owner Ionuț Radu (dentist, 43); wire is 12 m high, 18 m long, no net; Elena Stanciu (77) beside Ioana in row three. The page tears at "it was what he did when".

## Ch 37 — Exile Poems
- Hassan's clinic: second floor above a pharmacy, Giza; manager Hatim Babiker (superstitious, always late); cleaner Old Zuhura (70, from Aswan, illiterate, tapes Amal's poems on walls as "prescriptions").
- Hassan practises without a licence under a colleague's signature. Mother is ill in Omdurman; brother Osman (umbrella maker) sends broken voice notes. Backstory only hinted: "a boy of nine on a bad road".
- Amal's cycle: 31 poems in the future tense about Hassan, written since the Register opened; she read his Entry 0181 before meeting him in January; she leaves the poems every Thursday in the clinic bandage cupboard. The 31st (last) poem is in the present tense: she is standing in the doorway behind him. Torn page at "when he turned round she would".
- Nahid Osman (15, from Khartoum, lives with her aunt) attends the reading.
- Baghdad: a 600 m footbridge from the Mutanabbi Street book-market (Rusafa) to the Karkh shore, due to open in autumn, 2 m gap still open between the two halves. Noor designed the lamps, railings and Rusafa-end pavilion. Noor accepted the Turin scholarship (two years, leaves in September). Mustafa will leave one bolt untightened at the centre of the last span until she comes back and says it may be done. She threw him her pencil. Noor's father Hussein Al-Tamimi (lifeguard, 78) came by bus. Shaimaa Al-Obaidi (stonemason foreman), Zaid Jabbar (butcher-matchmaker).

## Ch 38 — The 5:40 to Lucknow
- Fictional station: Shanti Nagar Junction (eastern Delhi), platform six. Ravi's 5:40 to Lucknow passes at 5:58; horn code "two short, one long"; Sunita waves a yellow handkerchief; she carries a steel flask she never opens. Four years of this. First halt (signal turned red): she gave him cold morning tea; he said "I have always wished to stop." Afterwards the horn sounded a new, long, unrehearsed blast. Ravi believes she waits for some other man's train; Sunita believes he knows why she waits.
- Rohit Bansal (assistant loco pilot, reads Urdu poetry), niece Shweta Bansal (16, Sunita's pupil, prayed at the Hanuman shrine in the signal cabin), Pooja Singh (platform biscuit vendor).
- Vienna: Jonas's violin has a deliberately built-in wolf note (the "flat note"); Clara played it; she forbade him ever to fix it. Lukas Bauer is the last bus conductor in Vienna; Theresa Hofer is Jonas's apprentice.
- S20 is tragic: Entry 0032 exposed Nikolai through a colleague; inquiry by Christmas, charge by March, trial on 3 June. He admitted letting a child (Tsvetana Dimitrov, now 15, Plovdiv) cross nine years ago on 14 November at the mountain-road post. Sentence two years. Elena's forged passport was for Nikolai; he refused to flee. He died in week nine in the prison infirmary while reading Elena's French letter aloud to an illiterate cellmate (heart condition he never disclosed).
