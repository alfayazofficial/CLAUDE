# Continuity notes — Chapters 57–60

## Ch 57 — Telescopes Over Lima
- Sergio Castillo owns the cebichería **La Marea** (Surquillo, Lima). He watches the public all-sky camera of the university's mountain station "above Ticlio" (his "only telescope"). Lucía Paredes returns his weekly (Thursday) bowls via Milagros Ramos (tea-seller at the university gate) with star coordinates pencilled on the rim.
- Lucía's father (Cusco) taught her the Inca dark-cloud constellation Yacana (the dark llama); "yana quyllur" = black star. Her candidate "dark star" is **unconfirmed**; it must be seen twice, the second sighting due in **eleven days**. Open ending.
- Renzo (Sergio's brother, umbrella-maker, small cough he will not take to the doctor); Rosa Chávez is the comic fool.
- Carlos Mendes: pale ring-band on left hand, ring worn seven years, taken off "in the tango school" (matches outline ch22); Beatriz tells him "ask me on a Saturday on which I have slept"; Seu Gustavo (taxi). Open.
- Petr Novák speaks without a puppet for the first time ("I would like to be reviewed by you, for the rest of my life"); Lucie Horáková gives four stars; puppet Vendelín; ticket-seller Markéta.
- Sebastián finishes eleventh stage, stops for Daniela's carnation; bank gives her "until Monday"; Esteban Ortiz commentates. Open.
- Ali & Parisa marry in Isfahan (the bride "gone to pick flowers", *baleh*). Ali's four-year never-repeating wall pattern is finished.

## Ch 58 — Ten Thousand Miles of Wifi
- **Time-zone reconciliation:** Ermis is in Rotterdam (5 hours behind Dhaka, 6 behind Java). To keep the Friday appointment ("ten, his; eleven, hers") she keeps a wall clock in her Rotterdam studio set to **Java time** ("on Fridays Rotterdam is a province of Java"); the call is really 5 p.m. in Rotterdam. She now says she is "five hours behind" him and asks him to describe her "future".
- Fayaz now has a **first contract in his own name** (logistics firm, container-tracking system, daytime hours), replacing the earlier Singapore night-work through an agency. Debt: **four lakh taka left** of fourteen; his father keeps the green ledger (see ch14) and strikes through a number with a steel ruler on the first of each month; four numbers remain. His father is alive and "better" (assumes he survived the ch44 collapse); his mother's treatment is in its second phase. Tithi (now 18) has been admitted to university. He has an IPS ("the generator, on the right circuit"), and the Wi-Fi network is named BINTANG_HITAM (Tithi).
- Bunny is now 14 (cash drawer, not kettle). He unknowingly suggests meeting "in the middle of the sea".
- Ermis's final Rotterdam piece: **"Selisih"** (the difference), a 3-metre cloth in two halves (left: night indigo with one white star; right: pale dawn with one black star). She plans to **cut it in half** but has not decided "which half will be whose". Exhibition opens the next day. Her lab commitment: "until March", with a possible second year.
- Ayu the parrot has learned a new word: **"FAYYAAS"** (Fayaz's name), via Ermis's grandmother. Dewi (Ubud) teases about it.
- They agree: no date; "**when both our skies are clear**"; meet in **Phuket** ("halfway, where no flag stands"; Phuket is on Java time, so Fayaz will set his watch forward and they will be the same hour). Fayaz's line: "I would tell you everything" with the *if* removed. The word *love* is never said. Ends on both typing, neither sending.
- Toronto: Idris's mother (unnamed in text) and sister (age 15, unnamed) arrive at Pearson after three years; Fatima's parents came from Herat; Fatima speaks to Idris's mother in Dari (the sentence is not translated). Najib Sultani is the comic fool (baby-shower balloons).
- Lyon: Pierre and Margaux cut a door through the wall between bakery and florist; Pierre bakes a second loaf scored with violets; Mathieu the clockmender's clock strikes thirteen. They are effectively betrothed; no wedding shown.

## Ch 59 — The Last Postman of Cornwall
- Village: **Penhallow Cove**; Chapel Row, no. 3 (green door, brass fish knocker). Harry Whitcombe's last round on the last Saturday of October (the sub-post-office is closing; post-office gossip **Mrs. (Ruth) Trelawny**).
- Harry wrote forty letters, one a year on the night before Dorothy's birthday (14 March), never posted; the first dated 1986. Dorothy paid for forty hems/mends with one first-class stamp each and kept forty stamps in a jar. The collie dynasty "Boscawen" bites his left cuff because Harry put a sausage in the cuff in 1986. He will now deliver one letter a day for forty days.
- Harry carried all thousand Register letters up the cliff path to Lantern House over forty-five years.
- Four letters (Ji-woo/Daniel, Ravi/Sunita, Joseph/Grace, Takumi/Yuki) reach Lantern House. Ravi stopped the 5:40 at platform six and is to marry Sunita at the new year. Joseph and Grace married in the school yard. Daniel has resigned from his team.
- Edmund Ashby lives at **the old coastguard cottage** (Carrick Cove, two miles round the bay); he does not read his post; Nadeesha gives Harry an invitation for him for the new-moon night.

## Ch 60 — The Sixty-First Letter
- Nadeesha and **Anil are married** (wedding in Galle in the spring; the Galle lighthouse was lit for the guests). Anil relights the 1930s Fresnel lamp in the **lantern room of Lantern House**, dark for a year; Seraphine had lit it every night for thirty years ("Lit. Ships safe. He is at the window.").
- Edmund Ashby (81, nearly blind) took the cottage at the cove thirty years ago and saw her lamp every night; he never knocked. He came tonight because the lamp was relit.
- Seraphine's letter (Entry 1000) was extended from epilogue-draft.md; it still opens "You will say I am late…" and stops at "neither of us could bear to be the one who". Reading time 11:49 p.m. (eleven minutes before midnight, the hour she died).
- George and Eleni (Crete) will marry in spring after the pruning, Eleni in black. Eleni opened all thirty letters in a week. Héctor and Rosa (Havana): strangers who found his notes in cigar boxes wrote back (a Hamburg dentist, "Dr. K."); Rosa will visit her sister in Madrid for a month in the spring, then return.
