# Continuity notes, Chapters 51-56

## Ch 51 (Tea and Tide)
- S51: the dead worker is Jamal Uddin, 22, a cutter from the north, killed 11 months before when a hull plate fell after a gas pocket; Rahim had signed the permit.
- Jamal's widow is Rokeya (about 30, tea plucker near Sreemangal); daughter Mitu; Rahim has sent 3,000 taka by money order on the 9th of each month for 11 months, signed "R. Chowdhury, Fauzdarhat, Chittagong".
- Nusrat has been 4 years at the garden dispensary (only doctor for ~3,000 pluckers); her autoclave has been broken since June; Rahim offers to fix it; he stays to dinner (no engagement yet, only a warm opening).
- Nayeem (#0945) keeps the yard canteen at Sitakunda; Babul is an older yard hand (22 years).
- Naples: the wedding menu "concordat" (pizza first, pastry last, middle left open); Paolo Ricci is cousin to both families.
- Verona: Giulia and Marco marry in the courtyard of a small house; Marco sings his vow; Enzo Conti writes it on his cuff for "the wall".
- Split: Marija accepts the Rotterdam-Singapore chief officer's berth; her ship is the Zagora, Ivan's is the Vela; the agreed wedding date in Split is 9 March (4-day schedule gap).

## Ch 52 (Dancer of Ubud)
- Dewi's mother (Ermis's aunt, not named) is Javanese; Dewi grew up in Ubud; Ermis's grandmother is "Eyang" (80); Bayu Saputra (#0578), scribe, escorts her; Ayu the parrot travels to Bali.
- Putu dances baris (warrior dance); no Legong is described. Dewi weaves her own songket with poleng (black-and-white check) hem.
- Ermis has been in Rotterdam about 6 months; she flies to Bali for Dewi's wedding. Rotterdam is 4 hours behind Dhaka in early October; Bali is 2 hours ahead. She says this to Fayaz on a Friday midnight call.
- Fayaz keeps the Bintang Hitam batik hung on his wall (Bunny says it is "a map of houses without electricity"); his mother's new inhaler helps; the debt number "has begun to consider going down"; Tithi has an exam on the 4th.
- Four weddings the same Saturday: Meera and Arjun (Kochi, 9:00, Meera brings the poem book; Arjun reads the poem aloud), Joseph and Grace (Lusaka, 10:00, lobola, children Chisomo and Mapalo, Thandiwe Mumba is Grace's friend), Alessandro and Chiara (Naples, noon, cathedral square; sister Anna Gallo reveals she posted Alessandro's unsigned letter to Seraphine), Putu and Dewi (Ubud, 5 p.m.).

## Ch 53 (Engine Eleven)
- Engine Company Eleven is on Dorchester Avenue, Boston; Lt. Patrick Murphy; retired Captain O'Hara (74); the February fire was on Ashmont Street (a boy of 9 carried out, now in a choir).
- Ellen's brother Danny Walsh, 24, roofer, died 14 years ago (23 January, Box 244, Dudley Street) carried down by Eng. 11 crew under Capt. O'Hara; Ellen says the word "fire" aloud for the first time at the firehouse.
- Beth Murray flies the medevac helicopter (best friend of Ellen); Rev. Laura Hayes is a Somerville pastor and gossip; Mike Flynn teaches medieval shipping and has no boat.
- Lagos: Chidinma's show is in a converted server farm in Yaba; Emeka's 14 former staff now work for her house (Kemi, ex-lead engineer, runs the sewing room); Chidinma proposes publicly; Rev. Ngozi (Enugu) blesses it. Gold aso-oke dress lined with indigo.
- Kisumu: Otieno and Wanjiru marry aboard the boat Mama Atieno off Dunga shore; Otieno streams under his real name and face.

## Ch 54 (The Tallinn Choir)
- Mikhail (42) maintains the Song Festival Grounds stage shell; lives in his late parents' wooden house in Kalamaja; mother died three winters ago; sister Helena (Pärnu) has written him 41 monthly answer-letters.
- Katrin lost her singing voice after thyroid surgery two winters ago (nerve damage); she turns pages in Liis Mägi's women's choir; her mother Triin Saar is a Muuga dockworker; Toomas Rebane is the only man in the women's choir.
- The shell's focal point is marked with a pencilled star; a whisper there carries to the far rim of the field (William timed a cornet note at 9.5 seconds).
- Torn page: breaks mid-sentence as Katrin sings at the focal point. The outcome is never told; the Hartleys witnessed it and refuse to say (postcard to Mr. Pennywhistle).
- William and Poppy Hartley honeymoon in Tallinn (assumes Ch 49 marriage); Mr. Pennywhistle (68) has been band-room caretaker for 41 years.
- Jaipur: the Rathore haveli gets its roof; Priya's collection shown there; Vikram proposes; Priya accepts with a contract (north wing studio, courtyard on Thursdays, final say on colour). Pooja Tiwari is a magazine writer; Pankaj Singh cooked the dal.

## Ch 55 (Cassava and Code)
- Victor names his new mosaic-resistant cassava line "Funmi" (registered at the research station on the hill above Ibadan; director Prof. Segun Afolabi); cassava peel replaces 40% of maize in Funmi's poultry feed; her loan is 6 million naira; bank manager Mr. Tunde Obi, who was formerly a dentist.
- Lisbon: Tomás and Inês marry in a small church in Alfama on a Saturday in October; Pereira walks the bride; Nuno Pereira is best man; Inês reads the "second line" of the letter aloud (its words are never given).
- Vienna: Clara's orchestra tunes a quarter-tone flat at her wedding; Jonas plays the one wrong note.
- Gothenburg: Erik and Astrid marry on a rock in the southern archipelago; glasses with a deliberate bubble "pulse".

## Ch 56 (The Archive of Lights)
- Archive of Lights is at Bygdøy, Oslo; Ingrid Solberg (47) catalogues logs; the 1998 Havørn log (Bergen, 14 June, 21:40, "I. Solberg") was stolen into her drawer in 2001.
- Anders is 50, retired two summers ago, lives in Grünerløkka with a cat; Captain Dag Ellingsen (60) runs the sightseeing boat Fjordfrue; crew Astri Dahl (deckhand) and Ragnhild Dahl (engineer), all "parrotless". Final log entry 6 November 07:35, "Character: steady."
- St Petersburg: Anya dances the waltz while humming; Dmitri plays only the first sixteen bars.
- Addis: Samuel has paid 11 of 41 creditors (bank deadline June); Selam coaches 30 girls on Entoto from Monday; Samuel may "walk behind" (open).
- Red Sea: Ivan and Marija talk for 11 minutes off the Gulf of Suez; wedding date may slip to April or May (open).
- Bonwire: Esi accepts the scholarship (2 years); Kofi weaves one kente strip per month (24 strips) with a sankofa bird (open).
